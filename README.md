## 📌 About the Conference

The **International Conference on Next Generation Technologies for Sustainable Development (ICNGT–2027)** brings together scholars, researchers, academicians, scientists, engineers, industry leaders, and policy makers from around the globe. 

The conference places **Artificial Intelligence at the core** of next-generation technological solutions, exploring how AI, digital technologies, smart systems, healthcare innovations, and clean engineering can contribute to an inclusive, resource-efficient, and resilient future.

## 📚 Publication & Indexing

* All accepted full-length papers presented at ICNGT–2027 will be compiled into conference proceedings published by **Taylor & Francis (T&F)** in print and electronic formats with an **ISBN** for the book and **DOI** for each chapter.
* The published volume will be advocated for indexing in **Scopus**.
* Presentation abstracts (oral/poster) will be published in the official conference souvenir cum abstract book.
